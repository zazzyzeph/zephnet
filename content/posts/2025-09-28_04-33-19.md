Join us at Iffy Books in Philadelphia for our second Philly Homebrew Website Club! 

Homebrew Website Clubs are meetups where we discuss designing and developing our personal websites as a way to take back our online identities and control our content.

Common discussion topics include:
- Website design and development with HTML and CSS
- Progress and breakthroughs for attendees' personal sites
- IndieWeb standards and goings-on
- Retro websites and webrings
- Miscellaneous chit chat!

All skill / experience levels welcome, don't feel like you need to have a functional website to join in - just an interest in making your own home on the web!

That said, please bring a laptop :) Iffy Books has some loaner computers, but if you're planning on showing off your site or working on it during the meetup, it's probably better that you have your own laptop.
