Version two of my hydroponic planter setup is a go! It's an AutoPot 2 pot rig, and I reinforced my pvc enclosure's joints with duct tape and tied the whole thing to a metal pole in the back patio. Lessons learned from last time - the plants got too big and when it got too windy the pipes separated from the joints and the whole thing toppled over many times 😅
